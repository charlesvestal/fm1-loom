Install LOOM-8

Connect the FM-1 by USB, then press Install. It works in Chrome or Edge; the browser asks once to allow MIDI: allow it.

LOOM-8's sounds now live in the sample bank. From 0.8, its own sounds (the kits' drums, the instruments, the sweep and the vinyl: 86 KB) are no longer built into the firmware, to make room for Bluetooth MIDI. The installer adds them to the sample bank after your samples, which keep their numbers; if your samples leave no room for them, it asks first. The four breaks (from 0.7) are on the samples page: "Add the breaks".

Do not unplug the USB cable while writing.
