Install LOOM-8

Connect the FM-1 by USB, then press Install. It works in Chrome or Edge; the browser asks once to allow MIDI: allow it.

The breaks have moved. From 0.7, the four breaks (classic, heat, choke and ecstasy) are no longer built into the firmware. After installing, add them from the samples page with "Add the breaks". They then work as before, in the slice presets and in your projects.

Do not unplug the USB cable while writing.
