909 cymbal samples: hh.wav (the hat, closed and open), ride.wav, crash.wav.

Source: ER-99 by Matthew Cieplak (https://github.com/matthewcieplak/er-99), shipped unchanged by 9W9
(athousanddetails), copied unchanged from X0X's assets/909/. Licence: GPL-3.0 (ER-99 and 9W9 are GPL-3.0).

tools/gen_909res.py packs them into the resource region (fm1.toml resources), 5-bit block companded; the 909 hat,
open hat, ride and crash kit models (app/dsp/d909.c) play them.
