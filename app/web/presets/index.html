LOOM-8

Presets

LOOM-8 holds 64 user presets, U1 to U64, after its own sounds on the PRESETS button. A preset pack is a file of them (.syx): open one here and send it to the FM-1, connected by USB, in Chrome or Edge.

Making packs

From a computer, in LOOM-8's source: tools/loom_presets.py make PACK.syx SOUND.xy ... makes a pack from sounds written as the simulator's lines; tools/juno_import.py pack and tools/prophet_import.py pack turn a Roland Juno-106 or Sequential Prophet-5 bank you own into one, on LOOM-8's knit engine. A pack made by one version of LOOM-8 loads in later ones. Sending replaces the slots it goes to; the others stay as they are.
