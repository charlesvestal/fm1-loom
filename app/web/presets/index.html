LOOM-8

Presets

LOOM-8 holds 64 user presets (U1 to U64, after its own sounds on PRESETS) and 8 user kits (K1 to K8). Open a preset pack (.syx), a LOOM-8 backup (.loom8), or a Roland Juno-106 or Sequential Prophet-5 bank you own (.syx: its patches become knit sounds, here in the page) as a library, put the presets and kits you want into the FM-1's slots, and send them. Or save what the FM-1 holds as a pack to share. The FM-1 connects by USB; use Chrome or Edge.

Drag from the library onto a slot, or select (shift-click for a run) and click a slot: several fill the slots from there on. Presets go to U slots, kits to K slots.

Where packs come from

A pack holds LOOM-8's own presets and kits, any engine: saved from an FM-1 here, from a backup, or made on a computer with LOOM-8's source (tools/loom_presets.py). A Juno-106 or Prophet-5 bank is turned into knit sounds here as the source's tools/juno_import.py and tools/prophet_import.py do (the same sounds), each set to the level of LOOM-8's own pads; what knit can't do is listed beside it. A pack made by one version of LOOM-8 loads in later ones. Sending replaces the slots it goes to; the others stay as they are.
