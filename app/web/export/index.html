Export

Make a WAV file of a project's song, or of one of its scenes. Your browser plays it faster than real time, so you don't need the FM-1 for it.

  1. Save a backup of your FM-1 on the backup page, or press Save backup in the emulator.

What's in the file

The file is 16-bit, 44.1 kHz stereo, at the level LOOM-8 sends to USB audio. A song plays from its first scene to the end of its last, and a scene plays as many times as you pick. After the music ends, the file goes on until the reverb and the echoes fade out, for up to 20 seconds.

Every voice sounds in the file, so a busy song can sound fuller than it does on the FM-1. The samples in the backup play too.

For details on backups, see the manual's Saving section. The idea for this page came from Bloomy, gfcalvi's fork of LOOM-8.
