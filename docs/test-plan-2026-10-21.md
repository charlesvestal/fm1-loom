## 3. New things

- [ ] **Scenes 17-32**: Arrange, OCT+ then a white key: scene 18. OCT- back.
- [ ] **Scene in the header**: "S3" before the tempo on every page.
- [ ] **Kit sound mutes**: Arrange, hold T1's black key, white key 3: the hat stops on T1 only (a second kit keeps its
      hat). Let go: T1 not muted. Tap the black key alone: the whole kit mutes.
- [ ] **Per-sound velocity**: a kit step with kick and snare, hold it, black key for the snare, KNOB 1 down: the
      snare softer, the kick as it was.
- [ ] **Harmonic field**: a synth track, ARP, player strum: white keys from C give C Dm Em F G Am Bdim.
- [ ] **Copy/paste**: Steps SEL: COPY BAR, OCT+, PASTE BAR. Hold a step, SEL, COPY STEP; hold another, SEL, PASTE.
- [ ] **DOUBLE PATTERN** (Steps SEL): 16 steps become 32, the second bar a copy.
- [ ] **Bar jump / loop**: Bar page, hold SEQ, black keys 1-4 jump; two held loop.
- [ ] **Arrange KNOB 4** "T2 PAT": changes T2's pattern in the scene.
- [ ] **Arrange meters**: small meters beside each track while playing; COND / THROW blink on their steps.
- [ ] **Mix multi-solo**: hold two black keys: both soloed; let one go: the other stays; both: mutes as before.
- [ ] **Clear a knob's automation**: record motion (REC held, turn a knob while playing), then hold SEL and turn
      that knob: the motion gone. With a step held: only that step's.
- [ ] **Held step SEL list**: CLEAR LOCKS (last row) clears its locks; SAVE with a step held no longer does.
- [ ] **SEQ held on Steps**: OCT changes the keys' octave, PRESETS the sound.
- [ ] **Motion on the pictures**: record motion on Sends (FX I): the page's bar moves as it plays back.
- [ ] **LOOP THROWS** (GLO SEL list): "drift": a stutter let go stays out of step.
- [ ] **Weave**: a 2-bar pattern woven: OCT pages its bars; the rule reads "pitch".
- [ ] **Knit**: osc 2 at 0 st with level up, detune turned: the two beat.
- [ ] **MASTER knob**: turn it quickly while playing: no zipper.
