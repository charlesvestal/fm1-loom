## Rule one: no mdkit

The TRX and EFM drum models (ports of mdkit) were removed on 2026-10-23: their originals may be copyrighted, so they
cannot be distributed. They are gone from the code and from the unpushed history. A copy is kept outside every repo,
in `~/private-stash/loom-mdkit-2026-10-23/`: **do not open it, copy from it or port mdkit again.** Write new engines
from their own sources, with the source's licence checked first (and how that source was made, if it is a port), or
from scratch. Credit sources in the manual, as the other engines do.

## Where a new engine goes

- **A drum model**: last in `drumline.h`'s model list (saved by number: never renumber), then its row in each table
  in `drumline.c` (DM_NAME, DM_KNOB, DM_DEFAULT, SPEC: length range and trim, DM_NKNOB) and its cases in `start()`
  (the hit) and the render switch. A model may have up to eight knobs: knobs 5..8 are TP_DRUMX, shown on an Engine 2
  page (pages.c kit_x_page) when DM_NKNOB says more than four; DM_KNOB2 / DM_DEFAULT2 name them. Its pad name in
  pages.c pic_drumline (PAD[], a few characters), its words in app/words.tsv, a row in the manual's model table.
  `tests/host/drumline_test.c` sweeps every model's knobs (level, length, NaN): a new model is checked for free.
  A factory kit using it: ui.c kit_factory, pages.c KIT_NAME / NFKITS.
- **A synth engine**: synth.h's EG_* list (also saved by number: a new one before EG_DRUMLINE would renumber, so add
  it with care: PROJ_FORMAT and a migration if numbers move), its knobs in synth.c (EG_NAME, EG_KNOB, tp_info's
  ranges), the voice's prepare / render paths (voice_chunk's per-engine set-up, eng_chunk, voice_samples), presets
  in tools/presets/ (gen_presets.py), and the manual's engine table.
- **CPU**: drum voices render on the first core only, a voice at a time over the call (drumline_render); measure on
  the FM-1 with tools/demos/device_load.py (groups in device_projects.py: A, B, C, M) and on the Mac with LOOM_WORK=1
  (app/host.c). Hot code can go in RAM (tools/size_fns.py RAM_SETS; a call across flash and RAM needs a pointer the
  compiler cannot fold). 64-bit multiplies stay out of line (bsp docs/CPU.md); no double, no libm (fastmath.h).
